03Personal data

Booking request. The details entered in the form (first name, last name, email, phone, Instagram, profile, message) are not stored on a server: you send them yourself, by email or on Instagram, and they are only used to answer your request and prepare your session. They are kept for the duration of our relationship, then for 3 years at most.

Draft. The form keeps a draft in your browser (local storage) so you don’t lose anything. It stays on your device and is never sent.

Availability. The calendar only reads free or busy slots from the studio’s calendar. No data about you is sent.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to face@dake.studio. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
